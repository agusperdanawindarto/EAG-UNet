The publicly distributed package contains aggregate/per-image metrics, figures and a no-retraining notebook. Raw FIVES fundus images, annotations, and model checkpoints are NOT included. Download the FIVES dataset from its official distributor, observe its license, and use local checkpoints to regenerate Grad-CAM. DRIVE external transfer uses 20 official training-partition images and is not the DRIVE test benchmark. SHA256 content-group splits do not prove patient-disjoint partitioning.
